If you used Google today, you already used a brand new AI, and nobody asked you. Google just put Gemini 4 Hydrogen inside Search for every user in the world, and calls it the biggest model upgrade Search has ever had.

It went live on November 12th. Robby Stein, who runs product for Google Search, says it's now the default model in AI Mode, and it handles your hardest questions in AI Overviews, the AI answers at the top of the page.

The big change is how it reads your question. You type one messy question, typos and all. Hydrogen breaks it into smaller pieces and searches all of them at the same time. Google calls this query fan-out. Then it puts the answer back together with more links, images, comparison tables, and custom tools built for that question.

The surprise is which model this is. Hydrogen is Google's fast model, not its premium one. Google says it finishes tasks two and a half times faster than the model before it, and Google's own numbers put it level with Argon, its flagship.

The catch: all of this is Google's own claim. Nobody has independently tested the typo handling or the extra links yet.

So remember this. The search bar you've used for years got a new brain overnight, and it didn't tell you.
