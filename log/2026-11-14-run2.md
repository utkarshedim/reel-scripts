# 2026-11-14 (run 2)

## Today's pick

**A song with an AI-generated voice is No. 1 on Melon, South Korea's main music chart, ahead of K-pop idols. Korean media call it a first.**

- **Kind:** Buzzing. Korean outlets covered it within hours (Yonhap, cited by Asiae and Topstar; Sports Kyunghyang; Herald Business; eToday; Star News; SPOTV). It was posted on the aipost Telegram channel this morning.
- **Why it's today's reel:** Everyone gets it in one line ("no human sang the No. 1 song in Korea"). It broke in the last 24 hours, it's a different kind of story from the Nvidia scandal and Wallet that filled the week, and it has a sharp twist: the No. 1 song can't collect songwriting royalties. Easy to show: the Melon chart screenshot, the song's video, Suno's interface.

**Key facts**

- "꿈꾸던 어른이 되었나요?" ("Did You Become the Adult You Dreamed Of?") by MEOMURU (머무르) reached No. 1 on Melon's Top 100 as of 5 p.m. KST on 13 Nov 2026, passing RESCENE's new single "With." ([Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202611131743007/) · [Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080) · [Herald Business](https://mbiz.heraldcorp.com/article/10936693))
- It held No. 1 until 11 p.m. that day. ([eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125) · [Star News](https://www.starnewskorea.com/en/music/2026/11/13/2026111323071575352))
- Reported as the first AI-made song to top Melon's Top 100. ([Herald Business](https://mbiz.heraldcorp.com/article/10936693) · [Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080) · [eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125))
- Released 23 June 2026, so No. 1 came about five months later. It grew through short videos and word of mouth from September, was No. 3 on Melon in mid-October and No. 2 in late October. ([Topstar](https://global.topstarnews.net/en/articleView.html?idxno=16276335) · [Korea Times, 16 Oct](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/20261016/ai-assisted-songs-storm-korean-music-charts))
- The vocals were generated with Suno, a generative AI music app. People did the concept, the lyrics and the overall production direction. The album note says the vocals were generated with Suno. ([eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125) · [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/20261016/ai-assisted-songs-storm-korean-music-charts))
- The team regenerated the vocals many times, editing lyrics and syllables whenever the AI's Korean pronunciation or melody sounded unnatural. ([eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125))
- Behind it is the production team Glorywave, made up of composer-producers Lee Yong-seok and Choi Sung-hyuk, who have written ballads for Hwang Chi-yeul and Kim Jun-su. ([eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125) · [Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080))
- Melon listeners by age, per Yonhap: 30s 36%, 40s 30%, 20s 19%, 50s 11%. ([Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080) · [Topstar](https://global.topstarnews.net/en/articleView.html?idxno=16276335))
- The song reportedly isn't registered with KOMCA, Korea's music copyright society, so it isn't collecting songwriting, composition or arrangement royalties. Streaming revenue is settled separately. In August, KOMCA announced rules recognising human contribution to AI-assisted songs, then withdrew them. ([Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080) · [eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125))
- On the combined Music100 chart (six platforms), it is No. 2 behind A2O LITE's "NEON" (1,068.7 points to 1,083.5). ([Topstar](https://global.topstarnews.net/en/articleView.html?idxno=16276335))
- The creator's own words: AI is "a modern instrument and a partner" that turns imagination into reality. (Translated, [Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080))

**Unconfirmed or disputed**

- How much of the music the AI made is disputed. Every source agrees the voice is Suno's and the lyrics are human. eToday and Asiae say Suno also made the melody and arrangement; the Korea Times names only the vocals; one blog says Melon credits MEOMURU as composer. In the reel, say only that the voice is AI and the words are human, and that reports say the AI helped with the music.
- "First" is how Korean media describe it; Melon hasn't been quoted confirming it.
- "No. 1" is Melon's real-time Top 100 at a given hour, not a weekly or combined chart. On Music100 it's No. 2.
- The claims that Melon doesn't label AI songs and YouTube Music does come from a single blog (AI Musicpreneur): left out.
- Royalties: "reportedly" in every source.
