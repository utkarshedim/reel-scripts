# 2026-11-11

## Today's pick

**Tamil Nadu's public libraries bought books that appear to be AI-generated, with fake authors and made-up history; on 10 Nov the Madras High Court ordered a police case (FIR) and froze the publisher's bank accounts.**

- **Kind:** Buzzing. The row has run across Indian outlets since 6 Nov (The Week, The Federal, TNM, ETV Bharat, The Print editorial today, Oneindia's "India's first AI scam" explainer), and it took a real new turn yesterday: the High Court's Madurai Bench ordered an FIR, an account freeze and suspensions.
- **Why it's today's reel:** It's AI slop leaving the internet and landing on a government library shelf, paid for with public money, in India. The "wait, what?" is concrete and visual: a ₹1,445 book that calls an ancient poetry collection a person, a "history" naming a first chief minister who never held the job, and a book under economist Jean Drèze's name that he says he never wrote. The viewer leaves with a useful habit (check author and publisher before you trust a book), and there's plenty to show: the ₹300 crore library, book covers, the court headlines.

**Key facts**

- Writer **Perumal Murugan** published "Periyar Arivulagathil Periya Oozhal" (Major Scam in Periyar Arivulagam) on the **Sindhai** portal on **6 Nov 2026**. He counted nearly **1,800 books** from **Agal Vilakku Pathippagam** on the fourth floor of Coimbatore's Periyar Arivulagam, and **100+** from **Phoenix Pathippagam**. ([TNM, 6 Nov](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/fake-histories-dubious-authorship-perumal-murugan-questions-periyar-arivulagam-book-procurements-97) · [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/periyar-arivulagam-library-book-row-tvk-dmk-perumal-murugan-262155))
- He alleged some books "appeared to contain AI-generated content", with unverifiable ISBNs, fictitious author names, only an email as publisher contact, and inflated prices. ([The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/periyar-arivulagam-library-book-row-tvk-dmk-perumal-murugan-262155) · [TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/fake-histories-dubious-authorship-perumal-murugan-questions-periyar-arivulagam-book-procurements-97))
- Example: *Naladiyar Vaazhvin Vazhikaati*, 300 pages, **₹1,445**, describes Naladiyar as a "poet and philosopher"; Naladiyar is a collection of 400 verses attributed to Jain monks. Comparable editions cost about ₹350. ([TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/fake-histories-dubious-authorship-perumal-murugan-questions-periyar-arivulagam-book-procurements-97) · [The Week, 7 Nov](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/11/07/periyar-arivulagam-library-controversy-perumal-murugan.html))
- Example: *Tamil Nadu Oru Paarvai* names "Muthuvelu Subbaiah Iyer" as the first Premier of Madras Province in 1947, called "entirely fabricated" by TNM. ([TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/fake-histories-dubious-authorship-perumal-murugan-questions-periyar-arivulagam-book-procurements-97) · [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/periyar-arivulagam-library-book-row-tvk-dmk-perumal-murugan-262155))
- The library is a seven-storey, **₹300 crore** project (₹50 crore for physical books), inaugurated **16 Oct 2026**. ([The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/periyar-arivulagam-library-book-row-tvk-dmk-perumal-murugan-262155) · [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/11/07/periyar-arivulagam-library-controversy-perumal-murugan.html))
- Both sides say the books look AI-made: the TVK government accuses the previous DMK government of buying "AI-generated books with fabricated content"; a DMK legal-wing petition says the Agal Vilakku and Phoenix books "appeared to have been generated using Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools" and alleges **162 books** were published under one name, "Monica Ravi", in six months. ([The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/tamilnadu/battle-over-dubious-books-tvk-dmk-pass-the-buck-tn-govt-forms-panel-to-inspect-libraries/) · [DT Next, 10 Nov](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/dmk-moves-madras-hc-seeking-dvac-probe-into-periyar-library-book-scam))
- **10 Nov:** Madras High Court, Madurai Bench (Justices G.R. Swaminathan and M.D. Sumathi), on a contempt petition by advocate V. Ramasubbu, directed the DVAC to register an FIR, ordered the **bank accounts of Agal Vilakku, its proprietor Mohan and others frozen**, and directed disciplinary action including suspension. It said AI-generated and plagiarised books can fall under "doubtful taste" in the library rules, and that "Crores of rupees has gone out of the public exchequer." ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/litigation/madras-high-court-orders-fir-over-fake-ai-generated-books-in-public-libraries) · [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/high-court/madras-high-court/madras-high-court-book-procurement-scam-dvac-directed-register-fir-freeze-bank-account-558026))
- The court also told librarians to inventory suspect books and "make them unavailable to the reading public", and cited a book at Madurai's Kalaignar Centenary Library, *Democracy and People's Right in the Contemporary World*, credited to economist **Jean Drèze**, which he has denounced as fake. ([The Federal / PTI, 11 Nov](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-book-purchase-scam-madras-high-court-262490))
- The bench said "heads must roll" and told the Director of Public Libraries "Don't try to save anybody." ([TNM, 10 Nov](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/library-book-procurement-scam-madras-hc-tells-dvac-to-file-fir))
- About **2,000** Agal Vilakku books were removed from Periyar Arivulagam as the DVAC began examining records. ([DT Next](https://dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/dvac-begins-inquiry-into-book-procurement-at-periyar-arivulagam))

**Unconfirmed or disputed — say this in the reel**

- "AI-generated" is an allegation (Murugan, the government, the DMK petition, and the court's own wording), not a forensic finding. No investigation has published results; no FIR registration confirmed as of this run.
- The count differs: Murugan says ~1,800 books; the minister says ~1,500; DT Next says ~2,000 removed. Say "around 1,500 to 2,000" or "nearly 1,800, by Murugan's count".
- Who's to blame is a political fight: TVK blames the DMK-era purchase (orders Feb 2026); the DMK points to June 2026 date stamps after it left office. Don't take a side.
- The Jean Drèze book is a separate case at a different library (Madurai); don't say it's from Agal Vilakku or that it's AI-written.
- No response from the publishers has been reported. How much money was paid is unknown ("several lakhs" per the minister; "crores" per the court for the wider procurement).
