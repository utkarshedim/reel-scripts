# Script: AI-voiced song hits No. 1 on Korea's Melon chart (2026-11-14, run 2)

## 1. Research

Fresh check of the primary sources:

- **The track's own description on the music platforms**, quoted by Herald Business and Topstar: "the vocals on this album were generated using Suno AI technology", and "the concept, lyrics and overall production direction were handled directly by the artist Meomuru." The creator also says "AI is a modern instrument, a partner that helps bring my imagination into reality." Melon's page itself couldn't be opened from this run, so the wording comes through these two outlets.
- **The chart result:** No. 1 on Melon Top 100 as of 5 p.m. KST, 13 Nov 2026, passing RESCENE's new single "With" ([Herald Business](https://mbiz.heraldcorp.com/article/10936693), [Sports Kyunghyang](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202611131743007/), [Asiae citing Yonhap](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080)). It held the spot until 11 p.m. ([eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125), [Star News](https://www.starnewskorea.com/en/music/2026/11/13/2026111323071575352)).
- **The climb:** released 23 June; No. 18 on Melon on 2 Oct, No. 3 in mid-October, No. 2 in early November, No. 1 on 13 Nov ([Herald Business](https://mbiz.heraldcorp.com/article/10936693), [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/20261016/ai-assisted-songs-storm-korean-music-charts)).
- **The people behind it:** Glorywave, a team led by composer-producers Lee Yong-seok and Choi Sung-hyuk, which made the creative calls ([Herald Business](https://mbiz.heraldcorp.com/article/10936693), [eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125)). eToday says they've written ballads for established singers, and that they regenerated the vocals many times, fixing lyrics and syllables whenever the AI's Korean sounded off.
- **Royalties:** reportedly not registered with KOMCA, so no songwriting, composition or arrangement royalties; streaming money is settled separately. KOMCA announced rules for AI-assisted songs in August, then withdrew them ([Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080), [eToday](https://www.etoday.co.kr/news/view/2648125)).
- **Listeners:** 30s 36%, 40s 30% (Yonhap via [Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026111415270973080)).

## 2. The zest and the angle

**Zest:** The No. 1 song in Korea right now is sung by a voice that doesn't belong to any human, and it beat K-pop idols to get there.

The second question that keeps people watching is "so who actually made it, and who gets paid?"

## 4. Full script (about 100 seconds)

Right now, the number one song on Korea's biggest music chart is sung by a voice that doesn't belong to any human. It just beat K-pop idols to the top. And by the end of this, you'll know why this hit song reportedly isn't earning its writers a single rupee in songwriting royalties.

The chart is called Melon. It's Korea's main music app, and getting to the top of it is hard even for idol groups with huge fandoms.

On November thirteenth, at five in the evening, a song called "Did You Become the Adult You Dreamed Of?" went to number one. It pushed a brand new K-pop single down to second, and it stayed on top till eleven that night. Korean media say it's the first time a song made with AI has topped that chart.

Here's the part that sounds like a trick. The voice you hear, that emotional rock ballad voice, is not a singer. It was generated by Suno, an AI music app. The track's own description says it plainly: the vocals were made with Suno.

So who made the song? People. A production team called Glorywave, led by two composer-producers, Lee Yong-seok and Choi Sung-hyuk. They came up with the idea, they wrote the lyrics, and they directed the whole production. Reports say the AI also helped with the music itself. And it wasn't one click. When the AI's Korean sounded off, they changed the words and generated the voice again, over and over.

And it didn't go viral overnight. It came out in June, spread through short videos and word of mouth, and climbed slowly, eighteenth, then third, then second, then first. Most of its listeners are in their thirties and forties.

Now the catch. The song reportedly isn't registered with Korea's music copyright society, so it isn't collecting songwriting royalties at all. Streaming money is settled separately. In August, that society announced rules to cover songs made with AI, and then took them back. So right now, Melon's number one song sits in a grey zone that nobody has a rule for.

The creator calls AI a modern instrument. Whatever you call it, here's the fact to remember: Korea's number one song this week was sung by an AI, and no human singer was paid to sing it.

## 4b. Short version (about 35 seconds)

The number one song on Korea's biggest music chart right now is sung by a voice that doesn't belong to any human.

On November thirteenth, a song called "Did You Become the Adult You Dreamed Of?" went to number one on Melon, beating a brand new K-pop single. The voice was generated by an AI app called Suno. A two-person production team wrote the lyrics and directed it, regenerating the voice again and again until the Korean sounded right.

The catch? It reportedly isn't collecting songwriting royalties, because Korea's copyright society has no rule for AI songs yet. It announced one in August, then pulled it back.

Korea's number one song this week. Sung by an AI.
