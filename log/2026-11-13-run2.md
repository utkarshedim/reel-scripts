## Today's pick

**Google has put Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its new fast AI model, into Google Search for every user worldwide. Google calls it "the biggest model upgrade to Search yet".**

- **Kind:** Manual. Utkarsh picked it after the first run duplicated the Jensen Huang story, which another run had already made. It was also Buzzing on its own: HN 506 and 382 points for the two launch posts, and Google News India (ET CIO, Business Standard, India Today, APAC Media).
- **Why it's a reel:** Nearly every viewer uses Google Search, and the AI behind it changed on 12 Nov without anyone opting in. There are real things to show: the two Google posts, AI Mode answers with tables and images, and the "one question becomes many searches" idea.

**Key facts**

- **12 Nov 2026:** "Hydrogen is rolling out globally in Google Search to all users"; "the biggest model upgrade to Search yet". Search can handle "questions with multiple parts, hard-to-understand typos, or highly specific requests". The improved query fan-out runs simultaneous searches across subtopics for "a broader range of helpful links". Answers show "more images, structured responses, comparison tables, and custom tools built for you". ([Google, Robby Stein, VP Product, Search](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/))
- Stein on X: "It's now the new default model in AI Mode", and it's "being used for your hardest questions in AI Overviews". ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://seroundtable.com/google-search-gemini-4-hydrogen-42526.html))
- Model post: built for fast everyday coding and agentic tasks. It finishes tasks "2.5 times faster" than Gemini 3.8 Flash at the default high setting, and adds a fourth thinking level, xhigh. Google says it scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at high, vs 50 for Argon. Price is $1.50 / $7.50 per million input/output tokens. Free Gemini app users get it "via auto model selection when it's the appropriate model for the prompt". Google says it cuts overreach in agentic tasks by up to 81%. ([Google, Koray Kavukcuoglu](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/))
- Orchestrator in Antigravity: Carbon plans and Hydrogen does the edits and tests. On Terminal-Bench 4.1 it reached near-Carbon success with 24% less quota, 19% faster, for paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. ([Antigravity blog](https://antigravity.google/blog/gemini-4-hydrogen-in-google-antigravity/))

**Unconfirmed or disputed — say this in the reel**

- The Search improvements (typos, more links) are Google's own claims, with no independent tests yet. ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-search-gains-gemini-4-hydrogen-for-all-users-not-just-subscribers/))
- The Search post doesn't say which model Hydrogen replaces. PPC Land reports AI Mode had used Gemini 3.8 Flash since 2 Sept.
- The "51 vs Argon's 50" score is from Google's post. Third-party reports put Argon at 50 or 53 depending on the index version, so don't say "beats Argon".
- Reports that Google "retired the Flash brand" (BigGo) aren't in Google's posts. Dropped.
- India specifics: "globally" and "all supported AI Mode languages". No country list is given; AI Mode added Hindi in Sept 2025, per PPC Land.
