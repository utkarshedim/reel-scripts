# 2026-11-13

## Today's pick

**Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO, effective immediately, after 33 years, following reports of an affair with a woman US intelligence reportedly suspects of spying for China. She is a former executive at a company accused of moving Nvidia's AI chips into China.**

- **Kind:** Buzzing. It's the top story on Hacker News by far (3,122 points, 2,917 comments). Most of Techmeme is follow-ups. It's all over Google News India (Times of India, Deccan Herald, The Guardian).
- **Why it's today's reel:** Yesterday we passed on this because it rested on unnamed sources and the board hadn't decided. Now there's a primary source: Nvidia's own press release and SEC 8-K. The man whose chips the AI boom runs on is gone overnight, and the company gives no reason. The tech core is real: Nvidia's best chips are banned from China, and the woman at the centre worked at a company accused of shipping them there. There's plenty to show (the press release, the 8-K, Huang's apology email, the stock chart, the Senate hearing).

**Key facts**

- **12 Nov 2026:** Huang resigned as President, CEO and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and stays CFO. Harvey C. Jones is the board's first-ever chair. The board is searching for a permanent CEO, internal or external. No reason given. ([Nvidia press release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/) · [SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm))
- Jones: "we expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption." Q3 results come on 17 Nov. ([Nvidia press release](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/))
- **8 Nov 2026:** In an email to employees, Huang wrote "I owe my family and all of you an apology" and "I am deeply sorry". He said the board "has initiated an independent investigation". ([Nvidia, "A Message to Our Company"](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/a-message-to-our-company)) Per AP, the email says he was "unfaithful to my wife". ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-after-jensen-huangs-resignation-as-nvidias-ceo))
- Nvidia's board statement on 8 Nov: an investigation into reporting "that founder and CEO Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort". It has "no reason to believe that either US national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised." ([Reuters via Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/americas/nvidia-ceo-huang-foreign-intelligence-effort-affair))
- The NYT reported that US intelligence concluded Alice Huang (no relation), a former executive of Singapore's Megaspeed, was passing information to Beijing. Beijing denies it and calls it a smear. ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-after-jensen-huangs-resignation-as-nvidias-ceo))
- Megaspeed buys Nvidia chips and was accused last year of illegally shipping advanced Nvidia chips into China. It said there was "no evidence" of that. Singapore questioned Alice Huang, took her passport, found insufficient evidence of a crime, and barred her from returning. ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-after-jensen-huangs-resignation-as-nvidias-ceo) · [Reuters via Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/americas/nvidia-ceo-huang-foreign-intelligence-effort-affair))
- Nvidia shares fell more than 4% by Thursday's close. ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-after-jensen-huangs-resignation-as-nvidias-ceo))
- Trump called Huang an "honorable guy", said the FBI is looking into the affair, and suggested Tim Cook as successor. ([PBS/AP](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/what-we-know-after-jensen-huangs-resignation-as-nvidias-ceo)) Senate Banking Chair Tim Scott asked Huang and Lutnick to testify in December, and Lutnick agreed (Politico, per [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p70#a261112p70)).
- Context: Nvidia and OpenAI announced a plan to deploy at least 10 gigawatts of Nvidia systems to train and run OpenAI's models (Sept 2025). ([Nvidia](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2025/OpenAI-and-NVIDIA-Announce-Strategic-Partnership-to-Deploy-10-Gigawatts-of-NVIDIA-Systems/))

**Unconfirmed or disputed (say this in the reel)**

- That Alice Huang is a spy is a US intelligence assessment as reported by the NYT. There are no charges, and China denies it. US officials disagree on whether the intelligence proves it (CNN, per the [Techmeme headline](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p72#a261112p72); article not readable).
- Nothing reported shows Huang knowingly shared secrets. Nvidia says no secrets were compromised.
- A second relationship with an employee is from Reuters' unnamed sources ([HN link to Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/former-nvidia-ceo-huang-had-relationship-with-second-employee-sources-say-2026-11-12/)). Keep it out of the reel.
- HN carried a Wired story about an employee petition for his return. Wired was unreachable, so it's unverified and dropped.
