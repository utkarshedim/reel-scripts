## Today's pick

**Jensen Huang, who co-founded Nvidia, the company whose chips run the AI boom, resigned as CEO on 12 Nov, effective immediately. It followed weeks of reports tying him to a senior figure at Megaspeed, a Singapore customer under US criminal investigation for allegedly smuggling Nvidia chips into China.**

- **Kind:** Buzzing. It's the top Hacker News story by far ("Jensen Huang resigns", 3,308 points), most of today's Techmeme front page, and in Google News India ("Chinese AI stocks slide after Huang's Nvidia exit", Trump backing Tim Cook as successor). Nvidia's own 8-K confirms it.
- **Why it's today's reel:** The last two runs passed on this story because it was unconfirmed and the board hadn't decided. Now Nvidia has filed it with the SEC, so this is a real new development in that old story. Almost everyone has seen the man in the leather jacket. The tech core is real: Nvidia makes the chips that power AI, and the US limits which of them can go to China. There's plenty to show: the 8-K, the stock chart, Huang on stage, a map of Singapore and China.

**Key facts**

- **12 Nov 2026:** Huang resigned as President, CEO and director, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and stays CFO. Harvey C. Jones, formerly CEO of Synopsys, is the company's first-ever Chair. The filing gives no reason. ([Nvidia 8-K, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm))
- Huang apologised on 8 Nov for being "unfaithful to my wife" and gave no details. ([CNBC live](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html))
- Megaspeed is Singapore-based and "one of Nvidia's biggest customers in Southeast Asia". It is "under U.S. criminal investigation" for allegedly smuggling American-made chips into China against US export controls. Singapore is also investigating. Megaspeed says it takes export law "extremely seriously" and will cooperate. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/megaspeed-statement-nvidia-jensen-huang-alice-us-exports-chips-china-rcna608939))
- Alice Huang held a senior position at Megaspeed. Singapore barred her from re-entering for giving police inaccurate information, and said she was not investigated for espionage. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/megaspeed-statement-nvidia-jensen-huang-alice-us-exports-chips-china-rcna608939))
- Stock: opened down more than 13% on 12 Nov and closed down 4.3%. Market value has fallen about $2 trillion since the 6 Oct peak. ([CNBC live](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html))
- Trump posted that Apple CEO Tim Cook should take Huang's place. House Foreign Affairs Chair Brian Mast says Huang agreed to testify before Thanksgiving. Senate Banking Chair Tim Scott asked Huang and Commerce Secretary Lutnick to testify in December. ([CNBC live](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html) · [Politico via Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p70#a261112p70))
- Background: Huang, Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem started Nvidia at a Denny's in 1993. ([NBC Miami / CNBC](https://www.nbcmiami.com/news/business/money-report/jensen-huang-started-his-2-trillion-company-nvidia-at-a-dennys-breakfast-booth/3302554))
