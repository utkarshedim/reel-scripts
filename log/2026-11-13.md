# 2026-11-13

## Today's pick

**Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO after 33 years. He admitted an affair, and US intelligence suspects the woman, a former executive at a company that bought Nvidia's AI chips, of passing information to China.**

- **Kind:** Buzzing. #1 on Hacker News by far (3,336 points, 3,026 comments), most of today's Techmeme, Google News India ("Chinese AI stocks slide after Huang's Nvidia exit", Trump backing Tim Cook), and covered by AP, NBC, CNN, Washington Post and Variety.
- **Why it's today's reel:** Yesterday's log passed on this because the board hadn't decided and the reports came from unnamed sources. Now Nvidia's own SEC filing confirms it. It's the biggest tech story of the week, and everyone has heard of the leather-jacket CEO. There's a real tech core under the drama: Nvidia's chips run the data centres behind ChatGPT and Claude, and the question is whether those chips, and access to the man who sells them, ended up serving China. It's easy to show: Huang on stage, the filing, the stock chart, the Megaspeed reports.

**Key facts**

- **12 Nov 2026:** Nvidia 8-K: Huang resigned as President, CEO and board member, "effective immediately". CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and keeps her CFO role. Harvey C. Jones becomes the "first-ever Chair of the Board". The board is searching for a permanent CEO and will look inside and outside the company. The filing gives no reason and has no quote from Huang. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm))
- Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem and led it until Thursday, about 33 years. ([AP via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/12/business/nvidia-ceo-resigns-heres-what-we-know/))
- Huang's memo to employees (Sunday): "I was unfaithful to my wife." "I am deeply sorry." "My actions hurt her and our children." ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402) · [AP via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/12/business/nvidia-ceo-resigns-heres-what-we-know/))
- Timeline per NBC: on Sunday the board said it was investigating whether Huang had fallen victim to a foreign intelligence plot. On Tuesday it hired a former acting US attorney to investigate. On Wednesday the NYT reported a second relationship, with an executive who reported to him. Thursday, he resigned. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402))
- The woman: Alice Huang (not related), a former senior executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore company that bought advanced Nvidia chips. The New York Times reported that US intelligence suspects her of feeding information to Beijing. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402) · [AP via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/12/business/nvidia-ceo-resigns-heres-what-we-know/))
- Megaspeed is under US criminal investigation over claims it smuggled American chips into China. It denied this, saying there was "no evidence". Malaysia is investigating too. Nvidia says it follows US export law. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402) · [AP via Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/11/12/business/nvidia-ceo-resigns-heres-what-we-know/)) The NYT investigation put the hardware involved at about $2 billion. ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/singapore-company-alleged-to-have-helped-china-get-usd2-billion-worth-of-nvidia-ai-processors-report-claims-nvidia-denies-that-the-accused-has-any-china-ties-but-a-u-s-investigation-is-underway))
- Shares fell as much as 16% after the board's announcement and closed that day down 4.3%. They were down about 11% for the week through Thursday, roughly $500 billion in value. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402))
- Nvidia's chips supply "the data centres that run the models behind OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude." ([AFP via NST](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/opinion/columnists/2026/11/1575010/nvidias-huang-scandal-rattles-ai-industry-built-around-him))
- Trump suggested Tim Cook for the job: "A Legend replaces a Legend!" ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-reports-extramarital-relationships-rcna608402))
- Senate Banking Chair Tim Scott has asked Huang and Howard Lutnick to testify at separate hearings in December, and Lutnick has agreed. ([Politico via Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p70#a261112p70))
- Nvidia reports quarterly results on Tuesday 17 November, as already planned. ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000082/nvidiaannouncesceotransiti.htm))

**Unconfirmed or disputed — say this in the reel**

- The spy claim is unproven. China calls it "purely unsubstantiated and directed at smearing China" (NBC). US officials disagree among themselves, and some at the ODNI and CIA say the intelligence doesn't prove it (CNN via [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/261112/p72#a261112p72)). Singapore questioned Alice Huang and took her passport, but found insufficient evidence of a crime (AP). Singapore's home affairs ministry has also said it never called her an intelligence asset (per [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/singapore-company-alleged-to-have-helped-china-get-usd2-billion-worth-of-nvidia-ai-processors-report-claims-nvidia-denies-that-the-accused-has-any-china-ties-but-a-u-s-investigation-is-underway) coverage).
- No finding of chip leaks or security wrongdoing has been announced. Nvidia's filing doesn't link the resignation to the investigation.
- NBC hasn't independently verified the second relationship (the NYT's report). Keep it out of the script or attribute it.
- Market value figures differ between outlets (NBC ~$500B for the week; one search summary said $1.76T). Use NBC's.
- Whether Huang will actually testify isn't confirmed.
