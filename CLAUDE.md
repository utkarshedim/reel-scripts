# Reel scripts

Daily topics and scripts for Utkarsh's Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts on Utkarshhh.ai, an
AI and tech channel. A scheduled task runs `PIPELINE.md` every day; the scripts follow
`SCRIPT_PROMPT.md`, his own prompt.

- The viewers are curious everyday people with basic tech awareness, scrolling fast. The test
  for a story is how much it matters: the day's biggest AI and tech news, the kind a serious
  tech creator with 2 lakh followers can't skip, told so this viewer gets it. Viral curiosities
  are not reels.
- A reel tells one story. A story that needs five minutes of background isn't a reel.
- Every fact traces to a source that's linked in the log. No hype words, no numbers you can't
  trace.
