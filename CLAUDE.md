# Reel scripts

Daily topics, and later scripts, for Utkarsh's 60-second Instagram reels and YouTube Shorts on
TIN Tech, an AI and tech channel for an Indian audience. A scheduled task runs `PIPELINE.md`
every day.

- The audience is Indian viewers who follow tech. Global stories are welcome; the test is
  whether this viewer should know it.
- A reel tells one story in 60 seconds. A story that needs five minutes of background isn't a
  reel.
- Every fact traces to a source that's linked in the log. No hype words, no numbers you can't
  trace.
